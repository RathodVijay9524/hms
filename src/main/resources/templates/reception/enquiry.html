Frequently Asked Questions

What are the visiting hours for ICU patients?
Visiting hours for ICU are strictly monitored: Morning (11:00 AM - 12:00 PM) and Evening (5:00 PM - 6:00 PM). Only one visitor per patient is allowed at a time with a valid visitor pass.
Do you accept cashless insurance for TATA AIG?
Yes, we have a dedicated TPA desk and are empaneled with TATA AIG for cashless treatments. Please visit the Insurance Desk in the main lobby for pre-authorization.
Is a COVID-19 report mandatory for surgery?
As per current government guidelines, a negative RT-PCR report within 48 hours of admission is mandatory for all elective surgeries.
Hospitals OPD timing on Sundays?
OPD services are closed on Sundays. However, Emergency and 24x7 Pharmacy services are always available.
